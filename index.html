    What goes in

    Real fruit, a hard candy shell, and the candies and sauces you already love.

    • Fresh fruit

      Grapes, strawberries, pineapple, blueberries, blackberries, mango, cucumber and pickles.

    • Sugar coat

      A glassy candy shell in 25 flavors, from pineapple and lychee to vanilla ice cream.

    • Candy dust

      Crushed favorites like Jolly Rancher, Nerds, Skittles, Warheads, Lemonheads and Locochas.

    • Sauces

      Chamoy in four styles, sweet and sour, chili mango and tangy pickle. Or invent your own.

    • The Dubai line

      Milk chocolate, pistachio cream and crispy kunafa over fresh strawberries.

    Allergy note: some treats contain milk, pistachio and wheat (kunafa), and every candy carries its own allergens. Tell us about any allergy in your message before you order.

    Questions, answered

    Still curious? Message us on Instagram or Facebook.

    How do I place an order?

    Add what you want from the menu, open your order, and tap Send on Instagram or Send on Facebook. Your order message is copied for you, so paste it into the chat and send. You can also message @cvmdcandy directly.

    Where do I pick up?

    At Blackstone & Clinton in Fresno, CA.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes. Delivery is $5 within 10 miles, then $1 for every mile after that.

    What are your hours?

    10am to 10pm.

    What's the difference between sugar coat and candy dust?

    Sugar coat is the flavored candy shell around the fruit. Candy dust is crushed candy, like Jolly Ranchers or Nerds, rolled on for extra crunch and color. Not sure? Pick Mystery dust.

    Can I mix flavors or make my own sauce?

    Yes. Tell us your mix in the notes when you add a tray. You can also create your own sauce flavor for $1.

    Do you take custom orders?

    We do. Our Cucumber Tajín grapes started as a custom request. Custom tray orders must be paid in full upon arrival.

    What about allergies?

    Some treats contain milk, pistachio and wheat (kunafa), and every candy carries its own allergens. Tell us about any allergy in your message before you order.
